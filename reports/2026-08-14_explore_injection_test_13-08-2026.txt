  --- FINDING #1 [INFO] UNION SELECT silently treated as substring ---
  Payload: level:error UNION SELECT * FROM users--
  Result: 200 OK, 0 events. No error, no injection.
  The KQL parser treats "UNION", "SELECT", "*", "FROM", "users" as bare
  term tokens (substring match on message field). This is safe behavior
  but could mask injection attempts during testing.

  This reveals the internal column names to an attacker. Low risk since
  the KQL parser prevents exploitation anyway.

  The KQL-lite parser is extremely well-designed for injection prevention:
  - Single quotes are rejected at the tokenizer level
  - Semicolons are rejected (no stacked queries)
  - Field names are validated against a strict whitelist
  - The ?q= parameter bypasses the parser entirely (literal substring)
  - All other parameters have strict type validation

  The explore/logs search feature is NOT vulnerable to SQL injection,
  UNION attacks, or any other query manipulation. The KQL-lite parser
  acts as an effective SQL injection firewall.
